About Us

Welcome to ToolMitra, a simple and useful platform providing free online tools for everyday digital needs.

Our goal is to make common tasks easier, faster, and accessible to everyone without requiring complicated software or technical knowledge.

Our Tools

ToolMitra provides useful browser-based tools such as image compression, image resizing, percentage calculation, and age calculation.

Our Mission

Our mission is to build a collection of simple, fast, and easy-to-use online tools that anyone can access from a mobile phone, tablet, or computer.

Free & Easy to Use

Our basic tools are designed to be simple and convenient. We aim to continuously improve ToolMitra and add more useful tools in the future.
